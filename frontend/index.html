      FAQ

      Questions before uploading.

      What files should I upload?

      Upload the files required by the selected package: schedule, progress, cost, payment, material or risk records.

      How does Single Project payment work?

      You upload files first, review the mapping preview, then use Lemon Squeezy checkout to unlock the full dashboard and reports.

      What do Plus and Pro include?

      Plus includes 5 monthly project credits. Pro includes 20 monthly project credits for larger teams.

      Will empty dashboard blocks appear?

      No. The result view is designed to show only dashboard areas supported by uploaded project data.
