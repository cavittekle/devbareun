Built for practical project control

DevBareun is a construction analytics and project-control platform designed for contractors, developers, and project managers who need a clear view of what is happening on site.

Construction projects move fast. Schedules shift, costs change, materials run low, and decisions cannot wait. DevBareun turns project data into clear dashboards, schedule views, risk views, and recovery actions so teams can act before small delays become expensive problems.

With DevBareun, project teams can track progress, compare planned and actual performance, monitor cost and material continuity, identify critical activities, and prepare executive reports faster. The platform helps management see the real status of a project in one place without scattered spreadsheets, unclear reports, or delayed decisions.

DevBareun is built for practical project control: upload your project files, map the data, review the dashboard, and move directly to action.

What DevBareun helps you do

  • Track planned vs actual progress
  • Identify schedule delays and critical activities
  • Monitor cost and progress payment trends
  • Follow material continuity and site risks
  • Prepare executive dashboards and construction reports
  • Define recovery actions before delays grow
  • Keep project teams aligned with clear data

Why teams choose DevBareun

DevBareun helps construction companies save time, reduce reporting gaps, and make stronger project decisions. Instead of waiting for scattered updates, management gets a structured view of schedule, cost, risk, materials, and progress in one platform.
