About DevBareun

DevBareun is a construction project-control and reporting platform focused on executive project control, delay visibility, cost signals and risk tracking.

Mission

To help construction decision-makers move from scattered project files to clear dashboards, forecasts and management actions.

Core Modules

  • Schedule analytics and delay visibility
  • Cost variance and burn-rate signals
  • Workforce productivity indicators
  • Executive dashboard and report export

Built for

Project owners, construction managers, technical supervision teams, PMOs and development companies that need fast project visibility.
